Hello

About me

I've worked in IT since the early 2000s, starting out as a network administrator at a secondary school. Since then I've looked after networks and systems in schools, colleges, an ISP and a managed service provider, and later set up and ran an MSP of my own.

Today I'm a Senior Systems Engineer. Outside work I run the JacksonTechWorks YouTube channel, where I make in-depth videos about the technology I use in my job and the things I want to learn and share.

This site and everything on it is a personal project of mine, separate from my employer.
